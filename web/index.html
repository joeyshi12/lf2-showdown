LF2 Showdown

Choose your Little Fighter 2 v2.0a folder, the one with the data, sprite and bg folders. The files stay in this browser tab.

Play Little Fighter 2 v2.0a in your browser, on your own or against a friend online.

Controls

Player 1 moves with w a s d, attacks with j, jumps with k and defends with l. You can change the keys under control settings on the title screen. For a special move, press defend, then a direction, then attack or jump.

F1 pauses, F2 runs one frame, F4 returns to the menu, F5 changes the speed, and F11 and F12 change the volume. Click the game first so it gets your key presses and plays sound.

Network play

Two players can play over the internet. On the title screen, choose network game. One player chooses Waiting for Opponent and gets a room code. The other chooses Connect to Opponent and types in that code. The browser and desktop versions can play each other.

Download

The Windows and Linux versions are on the releases page. Download the archive for your system, unpack it, and run lf2.exe on Windows or lf2 on Linux.
